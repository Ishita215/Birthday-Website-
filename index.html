Dear Nani,

Happy birthday, my love. ❤️

I honestly don't know how to put everything I feel for you into a few paragraphs, because somehow you have become such a big and beautiful part of my life.

I hope this year brings you everything you've been wishing for. I hope you achieve everything you dream about, become the person you want to be, and have a million reasons to smile.

I am so lucky to have you. ❤️

Here's to another year of you, another year of us, and a million beautiful memories that are still waiting for us.

With all my love,
Kalki ❤️
