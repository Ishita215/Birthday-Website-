# Birthday Surprise Website ❤️

## How to open it
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
3. Click **Open your surprise**.
4. Scroll through the memories and click **Open my letter**.

Everything is local except the two Google Fonts loaded by the page. The photos and video are already included in `assets/`.

## Easy personalization
Open `index.html` in VS Code/TextEdit and change:
- The birthday message
- The captions under the photos
- The letter text
- "your girl" in the signature

The website is responsive and works on phone and desktop.
